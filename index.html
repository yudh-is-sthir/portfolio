QA Bug Report

🎮 GAME Little Sheep Valley
📅 DATE 16 Aug 2026
💻 PLATFORM macOS
🐞 TOTAL BUGS 3 Bugs Found
🔴 HIGH 1
🟠 MEDIUM 1
🟢 LOW 1
#01
HIGH

Character Animation Jitter

Walking and sprinting animations for the main character are not smooth — a noticeable jitter effect occurs, becoming more evident during sprinting. Highly visible to players; can break immersion and pull attention from gameplay.

REPRODUCE
  1. Open "The Valley" map (only available map).
  2. Start walking/running and focus the camera on the player's front view.
  3. Jitter is clearly visible during movement.
EXPECTED: Smooth, uninterrupted walking and sprinting animation.
#02
LOW

Tutorial UI Circle Misalignment

The tutorial highlight circle indicating the sheep's needs is positioned slightly above the actual UI area during the tutorial phase. Harmless but may confuse new players learning the care mechanic.

REPRODUCE
  1. Start a new game and proceed to the "New Sheep" unlocking step in the tutorial.
  2. Enter caring mode (go near sheep → press RMB). A red circle misaligns above the sheep needs area.
  3. Press E to continue — the "Affection" popup shows the same misalignment.
EXPECTED: Circle aligned correctly on the sheep needs area and affection hearts.
#03
MEDIUM

Sheep AI Trapped by Decorations

Sheep can be accidentally trapped using fence decorations placed near or inside the Shearing Station. Once trapped, the sheep cannot access food (leading to starvation) and blocks other sheep from entering the station.

REPRODUCE
  1. Wait for a sheep to enter the Shearing Station.
  2. Open Build Mode (press B) → Decorations tab → place a Simple Fence around the sheep.
  3. Sheep is now trapped — no food access, pathing blocked for others.
EXPECTED: Fence placement restricted near Shearing Stations to preserve sheep pathing.
